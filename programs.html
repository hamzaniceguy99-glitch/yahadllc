Four services for homes and small commercial sites, on a written schedule.

Program 01 · Cut at the right height

Mowing & grounds care

Most Florida lawns are scalped in summer, which stresses the grass and invites weeds into the gaps. We cut to the right height for the grass type and the season, keep edges clean, and come on the same day so you can plan around it.

Program 02 · Plants that suit the spot

Planting, mulch & beds

Plants fail because they were chosen for a picture rather than for the spot. We look at light, drainage and how much water a bed will realistically get, then plant accordingly — and we will tell you when something you have chosen will struggle there.

Program 03 · Shaped, not scalped

Hedges, shrubs & trimming

Shrubs trimmed at the wrong time lose a season of flowers, and hard-cutting everything into boxes slowly kills them. We trim to the plant and the season. Anything near power lines goes to the utility or a line-clearance arborist, never to us.
