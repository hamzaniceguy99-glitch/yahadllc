How we work

A Florida company, certified for what we apply

Yahad LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Florida, document number L21000149019, filed in 2021, with registration details on public record with the Florida Division of Corporations.

Applying fertilizer commercially in Florida requires FDACS certification, and many counties ban it outright during the summer rainy season — we work inside those rules even when a customer asks otherwise, because the restrictions exist to keep nitrogen out of the water. Pesticide application needs a licensed applicator. And we do not touch anything near power lines; that goes to the utility or a line-clearance arborist.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Will it be the same crew?

Yes, and the same day. Route consistency is most of what makes maintenance work, and we tell you in advance if a holiday shifts the day.

Can you fertilize in July?

In many Florida counties, no — there are seasonal blackout ordinances during the rainy months. We will tell you what applies where you are rather than quietly breaking it.

Why is my lawn thin?

Often it is being cut too short, watered too briefly, or both. We will tell you honestly, including when the answer costs us nothing to fix.

Do you remove trees?

Small trimming, yes. Removals and anything near power lines go to a licensed tree service or the utility — that work kills people who are not trained for it.

Do you guarantee plants?

No, and be sceptical of anyone who does in Florida. Survival depends on watering and weather after we leave. We will choose plants suited to the spot, which matters more.

How fast can you come after a storm?

Regular clients first, always. That priority is a real part of what a maintenance contract buys.

Yard getting away from you?

A free visit and a written quote, with no obligation.
